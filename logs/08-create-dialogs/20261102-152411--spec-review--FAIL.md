# Spec review — 08-create-dialogs

- Date: 20261102-152411
- Feature: 08-create-dialogs
- Phase: 1 (Spec)
- Result: FAIL

Verdict given by the author (spec validation is Manual). Findings were walked one at a time; the
author accepted every proposed fix. The six Summary decisions were not changed.

## Verdict

FAIL. One blocking gap: with a panel open, the dialog's "opened from the list" marker replaces
the panel's, so closing the panel afterwards leaves a dead history entry.

## Blocking

1. **One marker for two layers.** The marker is a single sessionStorage URL. Open a panel from the
   list (marker `?item=A`), click "New" (marker becomes `?new=1…`), Cancel the dialog (back to the
   panel, marker cleared), then close the panel: with no marker it replaces, and the next Back
   appears to do nothing (the bug spec 07 fix 2 removed). *Accepted fix:* the marker becomes a
   short list of opened URLs, one per layer. Opening adds its URL; closing goes back when the
   current URL is in the list and removes it; `clearOpenedMarker` removes only its own URL;
   switching rows replaces that panel's entry. Unit tests cover two layers; AC-12 gains: open a
   panel, open the dialog, Cancel, close the panel, and one Back leaves the list.

## Non-blocking

1. **Focus after "Create another" on Order Items.** Focus goes to the first field, Order, which is
   kept filled. *Accepted fix:* focus the first field that was cleared (Product on Order Items;
   unchanged elsewhere); AC-5 checks focus is on Product.
2. **Panel and dialog together on narrow screens.** Below 1024px the panel is a modal sheet that
   covers the "New" button, and a URL with both `?item` and `?new=1` would stack two modal
   layers. *Accepted fix:* decision 6 applies from 1024px up; below that, with both keys, only the
   dialog shows and the sheet returns when it closes; AC-12 gains an 800px-wide check.
